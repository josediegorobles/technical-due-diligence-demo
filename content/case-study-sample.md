# Anonymized rescue review sample

A founder is evaluating whether to rescue a stalled B2B SaaS platform after an
agency handoff. The review focuses on the repository, deployment path, dependency
risk, testing gaps, and the first 30 days of engineering stabilization.

This is an anonymized sample, not a customer claim. It shows the shape of the
evidence pack without naming a company, repository, investor, or commercial
outcome.

The output is a concise risk memo, evidence register, prioritized remediation
plan, and go / pause / proceed-with-controls recommendation.
